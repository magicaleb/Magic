Quick Start

For NO, begin drawing the next hangman part in the upper area. For YES, begin writing the letter in the lower area.

All strokes started in the same area within a short pause count as one answer group. The tree advances once per group.

While your finger is touching the canvas, the next letter appears in the Solve button. The final word appears faintly beneath the buttons.

Undo removes the complete previous answer group. Solve removes the lower YES writing and exits performance mode. Clear empties the board.

Covert Inputs

Length bucket: before starting, hold Hangman, slide into the left, middle, or right third of the screen, and release for Short, Medium, or Long.

Distinct vowels: before starting, hold Solve, slide left, middle, or right, and release for 1, 2, or 3+ different vowels from A, E, I, O, U.

Hidden Controls

Double-tap the top-left corner for Help. Double-tap the top-right corner for Settings. Those taps never draw or advance the tree.
