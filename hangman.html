Quick Start

Tap Hangman to begin. The first question appears briefly near the bottom of the board.

Ask whether that letter appears anywhere in the spectator's word.

For NO, begin drawing the next hangman part in the upper area. For YES, begin writing the letter in the lower area.

All strokes started in the same area within a short pause count as one answer group. The tree advances once per group.

While your finger is touching the screen, the next letter—or final word—appears secretly near the bottom controls. Remember it before lifting your finger.

Undo removes the complete previous answer group and returns to the previous question.

Solve removes the lower YES writing and exits performance mode. Clear empties the board and exits performance mode.

Hidden Controls

Double-tap the top-left corner for Help. Double-tap the top-right corner for Settings. Those corner taps never draw or advance the tree.
